Privacy Policy / Chính sách quyền riêng tư

Phi tiêu ngọt ngào (Sweet Hit)

Last updated / Cập nhật lần cuối: 15/05/2026

1. Introduction / Giới thiệu

Phi tiêu ngọt ngào ("we", "our", or "the game") is a casual mobile game developed by Enetty. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you play our game.

Phi tiêu ngọt ngào là game casual do Enetty phát triển. Chúng tôi tôn trọng quyền riêng tư của bạn và cam kết bảo vệ thông tin cá nhân. Chính sách này giải thích cách chúng tôi thu thập, sử dụng và bảo vệ dữ liệu khi bạn chơi game.

2. Information We Collect / Thông tin chúng tôi thu thập

2.1 Game Data / Dữ liệu game

The game may store the following data locally on your device or in the cloud (if you sign in):

  • Game scores and high scores
  • Game progress and unlocked levels or themes
  • In-game settings (sound, language, etc.)

2.2 Device Information / Thông tin thiết bị

We may collect non-personal technical information, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • General language and region settings

2.3 Analytics Data / Dữ liệu phân tích

We may use Google Analytics for Firebase (or similar analytics tools) to understand how players interact with the game. This may include:

  • Gameplay sessions and duration
  • Feature usage
  • General location (country or region level)

2.4 Crash Data / Dữ liệu sự cố

We may use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and error logs to improve game stability. This data may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Crash traces and error messages

2.5 Advertising Data / Dữ liệu quảng cáo

The game may display ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Ad interaction and impression data

Advertising data is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information / Cách sử dụng thông tin

We use collected data to:

  • Provide and maintain game functionality
  • Save your progress and scores
  • Display advertisements (where applicable)
  • Analyze usage to improve gameplay and fix bugs
  • Detect and resolve crashes and technical issues

4. Data Storage and Security / Lưu trữ và bảo mật

Your data may be stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) or locally on your device. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS where applicable.

5. Third-Party Services / Dịch vụ bên thứ ba

The game may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
  • Google Play Games / Game Center (if leaderboard features are enabled)

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing / Chia sẻ dữ liệu

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data may be shared only with trusted third-party services necessary for game operation, analytics, and advertising, as described in this policy.

7. Children's Privacy / Quyền riêng tư trẻ em

Phi tiêu ngọt ngào is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights / Quyền của bạn

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Reset ad identifiers through your device settings
  • Uninstall the game at any time

9. Changes to This Policy / Thay đổi chính sách

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
